# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

Sotto does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- The audio of a tab you control is processed **locally, in memory**, inside your browser, and played straight back to your speakers. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
- Sotto makes **no network requests** and includes no analytics, tracking, or remote code.
- The only tab information Sotto reads is the title and icon of tabs you choose to control, and only to show them in the popup. This information stays in memory and is discarded when you release the tab.

The source code is public, so anyone can verify these statements.

If you have questions, please open an issue in this repository.
